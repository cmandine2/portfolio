faq:
  - question: Do I need an Apple Watch?
    answer: No, the app works either way. Without a watch, you log your bedtime and wake-up time by hand, and the app rebuilds the summary of your night. With an Apple Watch, your nights and the times you got up are read automatically.
  - question: My nights are not showing up. What should I do?
    answer: Check that the app is allowed to read your sleep. Open Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Acalme, then turn on sleep and steps. Also check that the Health app actually contains nights. Without sleep data in Health, you can log your bedtime and wake-up time by hand in the app.
  - question: Is my data synced between my devices?
    answer: By default, your journal stays on your iPhone. You can choose to back it up to your iCloud, so you can get it back if you change or lose your phone. The setting is in the app, under Settings › Setup › "Back up to my iCloud", and it applies at the next launch. What you tap on the Apple Watch is sent to your iPhone.
  - question: How do I delete all my data?
    answer: You can delete your entries in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on the device. If you had turned on the iCloud backup, also delete the copy in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Acalme.
compatibility:
  - iPhone · iOS 18 or later
  - Apple Watch · watchOS 10.6 or later (optional)
note: Acalme is not a medical device. It helps you observe and document your nights; it makes no diagnosis and does not replace the advice of a healthcare professional.
