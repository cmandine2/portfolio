## Who is responsible

Acalme (iPhone, Apple Watch and widgets) is published by Amandine Cousin, who lives with restless legs syndrome (RLS) and built the app for her own nights. Contact: [contact@amandinecousin.com](mailto:contact@amandinecousin.com).

Since I have no access to any of your data (see below), I do not "collect" it in the regulatory sense: it is processed on your device, under your control.

## The data the app uses

What you enter in the app (your journal):

- bedtimes, wake-up times, awakenings, naps and times you got up;
- episodes (time, intensity, limbs affected);
- factors from your day (habits) and free-form notes;
- treatments and doses you report;
- results of the RLS severity test;
- your first and last name, if you provide them, only for the header of the sleep diary PDF.

What the app reads from Apple's Health app, with your permission, read-only:

- your sleep and your steps (to analyse your nights and spot the times you got up at night);
- optionally, your period dates and your workouts (to set them against your episodes).

The app never writes to Health. You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

What the app computes: the analyses of your journal, and the moon phase (calculated from the date, without location).

The app does not use your location, your advertising identifier, your contacts or your camera.

## Where your data is stored

**On your iPhone.** The journal is saved in the app. A shared space on the same device lets the widgets display your information.

**Between your iPhone and your Apple Watch.** The markers you tap on the watch (bedtime, episodes) are sent to your iPhone over the connection built into iOS and watchOS.

**In your iCloud, only if you choose so.** On first launch, the app asks whether you want to back up your journal to your iCloud; the default answer is "This iPhone only". If you accept, the journal is copied to your private iCloud database (CloudKit), so you can get it back if you change or lose your phone. This database is tied to your Apple Account: I have no access to it. Your data there is protected by Apple in transit and on its servers. You can change your mind in Settings › Setup › "Back up to my iCloud"; the change applies at the next launch.

**The iPhone's iCloud Backup.** If you have turned on iCloud Backup for your iPhone in the iOS settings, iOS may include the app's data in that backup. It is managed by Apple, independently of the app.

## Artificial intelligence

The written summaries and answers in the Analysis tab rely, when your iPhone supports it, on the language model built into iOS (Apple Intelligence). It runs on your device: your journal is not sent to any server to produce these texts. On a device that does not offer it, the app uses rule-based texts, also generated on the device.

## Sharing with third parties

I do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

- Exports (for example the sleep diary PDF) are created at your request; they go wherever you choose to send them, for example to your doctor.
- Some screens offer links to public medical sources (ameli, PubMed, etc.). They open in your browser, and those sites have their own rules.
- Apple handles whatever falls under its own services (App Store, TestFlight, iCloud, Health) according to its own privacy policy.

## Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you keep it in the app. You can delete entries in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes the local data from the device. If you had turned on the iCloud backup, the copy stays in your iCloud until you delete it: iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Acalme.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, port, restrict and object to the processing of your data. Since your data is on your device (and in your iCloud if you chose so), you exercise these rights directly by viewing, editing or deleting it. For any question, write to me at [contact@amandinecousin.com](mailto:contact@amandinecousin.com). You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).

## Not a medical device

Acalme helps you observe and document your nights. It makes no diagnosis, recommends no treatment and does not replace the advice of a healthcare professional. Talk to your doctor before any medical decision.

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated, and the app will let you know if the change affects how your data is used.
